# Data

This directory contains the raw and processed data used in the Open Sourcing Mental Health analysis.

## Structure

- `raw/` — original Open Sourced Mental Health survey datasets used by the ETL notebook.
- `processed/` — datasets generated by the analytical pipeline.

## Workflow

The project follows a sequential analytical pipeline:

```text
Raw OSMH survey data
        ↓
1. ETL
        ↓
cleaned_data.csv
        ↓
2. Exploratory Data Analysis
        ↓
3. Dimensionality Reduction
   ├── full_data.csv
   └── PC4_loadings.csv
        ↓
4. Clustering & Segmentation
        ↓
clustered_data.csv
        ↓
5. Propensity Modeling

Generated datasets are stored in data/processed/ and are excluded from Git.
